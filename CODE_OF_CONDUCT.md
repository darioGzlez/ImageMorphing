# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Project participants must communicate respectfully, welcome constructive
feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discriminatory
language, personal attacks, and publishing private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
